How is my GPA calculated?

Each letter grade has a point value. Multiply that value by the course’s credit hours to get its quality points. Your semester GPA is total quality points divided by total GPA credit hours.

Cumulative GPA = (previous GPA × previous GPA hours + semester quality points) ÷ (previous GPA hours + semester credits).

This calculator uses the 4.00 scale shown below. Only enter courses that count toward GPA. Pass/fail, withdrawals, transfer credit, and repeated courses may be treated differently by your school. Results are estimates based on your inputs, rounded to two decimal places.
